# About Me

I am a Lecturer at the [Ghulam Ishaq Khan Institute of Engineering Sciences and Technology (GIKI)](https://giki.edu.pk/), where I teach core computer science courses to undergraduate students. I received my B.E. in Computer System Engineering from the [National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST)](https://nust.edu.pk/) and my M.S. in Computer Science from [School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (SEECS)](https://seecs.nust.edu.pk/), NUST, Islamabad, Pakistan. I also completed a research fellowship at the [AI4EO Lab](https://ai4eo.de/), Technical University of Munich (TUM), Germany, in 2024, where I worked with multimodal datasets, that shaped the direction of my subsequent research work.
